Screening and Recruitment
The screening block is simulated too, because it is what separates Ntotal (recruited) from N (retained). Candidates who fail are discarded and replaced, so the retained cohort is always the requested size – what changes is the recruitment cost and the quality of who remains:

Screening thresholdPass rateCandidates per retained subjectMedian test-retest of retained
None (control)100%1.000.154
ρ > 071.4%1.400.204
ρ > 0.142.0%2.380.271
ρ > 0.222.9%4.370.340

The trade is steep and non-linear. Screening at ρ > 0.1 raises the retained cohort's median reliability by 76% over no screening, but requires recruiting 2.4× as many people; at ρ > 0.2 the cost rises to 4.4× for a further 25% gain. Retaining N=50 at ρ > 0.1 therefore means recruiting Ntotal≈119.
